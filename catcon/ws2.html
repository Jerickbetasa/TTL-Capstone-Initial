DESIGNING AND DEVELOPING INCLUSIVE INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIAL

PURPOSE: This worksheet guides your group in designing and developing the TEIM based on the proposal blueprint created in Worksheet 1. The focus is on modeling inclusive learning by designing instructional material that integrates technology meaningfully and supports diverse learners.

        After identifying the instructional context, learning outcomes, and evidence of learning, your group must design instructional material that enables learners to interact meaningfully with content.

        In the TUGMA instructional design framework, this stage focuses on: M - Model Inclusive Learning (Magdisensyo ng Ingklusibong Pagkatuto).

        At this stage, teachers create instructional material that demonstrates how learning can occur through clear content presentation, structured learner interaction, and inclusive design features.

        An effective technology-enhanced instructional material should:

  • present clear content and learning guidance;
  • provide interactive opportunities for learners;
  • support diverse learning needs and abilities;
  • integrate technology to enhance engagement and understanding; and
  • promote equitable access to learning opportunities.

Particularly, this will help you answer the essential question: How can teachers design instructional material that models inclusive learning while integrating technology to support learner engagement and understanding?

By completing this worksheet, your group should be able to:

  1. design a technology-enhanced instructional material aligned with the proposal blueprint;
  2. model inclusive learning through instructional material design;
  3. integrate technology that supports learner interaction and understanding; and
  4. ensure alignment between learning outcomes, instructional material, and technology use.

This worksheet develops your capacity as a creative and inclusive instructional developer by guiding you to design a TEIM that models inclusive learning and promotes meaningful learner engagement.

Part IV - Modelling Inclusive Learning (Magdisenyo ng Ingklusibong Pagkatuto)

Step 1 - Define the Structure of the Instructional Material

Using the proposal blueprint, develop and design the structure of the instructional material that learners will use during the learning experience. Focus on how the material presents content, supports learner interaction, and guides learners toward achieving the intended learning outcomes.

INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIAL STRUCTURE

Section of the Material

Content/ Description

Purpose for Learning

Introduction

Introduces the material through the Poly-lock theme, where learners are called Math Strikers. Presents the rules, learning goals, and pre-test, including the  Building the Teams section where learners are tasked to complete 2 missions to assess their skills. The first mission focuses on identifying the parts of the polynomial, while the second mission focuses on constructing polynomials.

To engage the learners through the Poly-lock theme, familiarize them with the rules and goals, and assess their skills and prior knowledge.

Content Presentation

Presents the concepts of Special Products and Factoring of Polynomials including the formulas, examples, and the step-by-step procedures on how to solve them.

To help learners to understand the concepts and learn how to solve the given problems.

Guided Activity

It is a teacher-guided activity during the learning process, which serves as a form of scaffolding.

To provide learners temporary support to master  new concepts allowing them to practice it independently later on.

Practice Activity

After each lesson, there is a practice activity with five items for learners to answer, followed by an answer key.

To give learners an opportunity to practice solving and  apply the procedures given in the lesson.

Reflection or Synthesis

It is a short assessment that needs to be completed by  learners after conceptual instruction and guided practice.

To evaluate learners’ skills, knowledge, and overall progress; to help teachers identify learning gaps so they can provide targeted strategies for students who need extra support.

Processing Questions

  1. How does the structure of the material guide learners through the learning process?

The structure of material guides learners through each part of the lesson, from the introduction, pre-test and pre-activity to the proper discussion, guided and practice activities, and final assessment. This sequence helps learners to understand and apply the concepts in an organized way.

  1. How does the material support active engagement among learners?

The material supports active engagement among learners by providing narrative-based learning and gamified activities, which capture the learners’ curiosity and interest. It also turns complex concepts and lessons into interactive and entertaining learning to reduce pressure on learners.

Step 3 - Model Inclusive Learning through Accessible Design

Identify strategies that ensure the instructional material supports diverse learners and promotes inclusive participation.

INCLUSIVE DESIGN FEATURES

Design Dimension

Strategy/ies Applied

Accessibility

The material uses visual patterns, clear instructions, and comprehensive examples to provide guidance to students on the different topics presented. The content is arranged from simple to complex and presented in a concise and consistent way, allowing students to process and understand the information properly.

Gender Inclusivity

The material avoids associating mathematical abilities with gender and uses gender-neutral roles by using “striker” as a representation of students. Although characters featured in the theme are male, the design encourages students to comprehend and demonstrate their mathematical skills. Success is measured based on their willingness to learn and evolve rather than their gender.

Cultural Relevance

The material uses activities and approaches that reflect Filipino classroom experiences. The theme is inspired from the Japanese concept of Blue Lock but it adapts to the students’ learning environment. It uses examples that relate to students while keeping the motivational quotes and elements of the anime within the mathematical content.

Learner Diversity

The material acknowledges students who struggle recalling terms used in the lesson. Also, other references and learning materials are included at the end of the material that students may use in support of their learning. Students may answer the practice items inside the material for easy mastery of the topic.

Engagement Strategies

The material contains practice exercises in between lesson topics covered by the learning competency and provides the students with the answer keys to check their own work and reflect for understanding. Additionally, adapting the concept of Blue Lock, it allows the students to learn through an experience of being a character trying to overcome different kinds of mathematical challenges.

Processing Questions

  1. How does the instructional material support learners with different needs and backgrounds?
  • The instructional material serves as a platform that supports students in having a progressive experience where every learner has the opportunity to enter, improve, and evolve. The concepts are comprehensively discussed and arranged from easy to difficult in order to gradually increase students' understanding. The concept of “striker” emphasizes equality, as it does not measure gender, background, or unnecessary attributes in gaining knowledge. Visuals, clear instructions, and guided examples help students with different learning needs to catch up and understand the topics.

Inclusiveness comes from recognizing that students are not expected to learn in the same way or at the same pace. It emphasizes that everyone has their own flow state and formula to evolve as a student.

  1. What design features ensure equitable access to the learning experience?
  • The material is in presentation form where it is most accessible to students and easy for them to navigate. Buttons are also provided per page to guide them throughout the whole presentation where each button checks the current learning situation of the student. When clicked, it directly leads them to the page where the student shall proceed with the lesson. Additionally, in each page there are fewer words so students will not be overwhelmed by the information provided.

Moreover, the design itself adapts with a theme much relatable to the students which can help in keeping them engaged and interested in learning the topics inside the instructional material.

GROUP REFLECTION

What was the most challenging part of designing the TEIM, and why?

Based on the overall process of designing the TEIM, the possible challenging part that the group had encountered was how to balance the pedagogy and the engagement of the content itself with the integration of anime-inspired Blue Lock, and the inclusive mathematical instruction. While integrating these engaging concepts capture students' interest in learning things, the group still had to ensure that it will deliver the lesson meaningfully and target the learning needs of the students in the particular topics rather than getting distracted from the theme. Additionally, it will be used as a tool to cater the diverse learning needs of the students through multiple guided practices and experience, while maintaining the learning goals.

What feature of your instructional material best supports inclusive participation and learner engagement?

Though all features support inclusivity, the feature of the instructional material that best supports inclusive participation and learner engagement is the way the theme itself is presented and the guided practices that can motivate students to progress and move further along the topics. The concepts are arranged to progression, which implies that students learn deeply about the topic as they move along, with the integration of guided practices that tests student knowledge on each topic, visuals for high engagement, and immersing the experience of being the persona in Blue Lock until the goal is achieved which adds the motivation of the learners to be curious and continue to find by exploring the topics itself.
