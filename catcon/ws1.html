Technology-Enhanced Instructional Material (TEIM)

The Technology-Enhanced Instructional Material[1] (TEIM) is an instructional resource that integrates digital technologies with sound pedagogical strategies to support meaningful and engaging learning experiences.

        An ideal TEIM demonstrates the following features:

  • alignment with curriculum standards and learning outcomes;
  • meaningful integration of technology with pedagogy and content (TPACK);
  • interactive and engaging learning experiences;
  • inclusive and accessible design for diverse learner contexts;
  • creative and well-organized presentation;
  • ethical and responsible use of digital resources and technologies; and
  • contextual relevance to learners’ experiences and learning environment.

To support pre-service teachers in designing effective TEIM, this worksheet packet provides a structured set of activities to guide the conceptualization, development, and evaluation of a technology-enhanced instructional material. The worksheets follow the stages of TUGMA (Targeting the Context, Understanding the Outcomes, Generating Evidence of Learning, Model Inclusive Learning, Assess, Adjust, and Advance) instructional design framework developed by G. C. Areta (2026), guiding students through context analysis, instructional design, and reflective evaluation.

The Conceptual Progression of the TEIM

  1. Contextual and Instructional Design Planning (Worksheet 1) – This worksheet focuses on understanding the instructional context and establishing a clear foundation for the instructional material. Students analyze learner characteristics, the learning environment, and the instructional setting to identify relevant needs and constraints. They then clarify the intended learning outcomes, determine appropriate evidence of learning, and explore possible technologies that can support achievement of the learning competencies.
  2. Designing and Developing Inclusive Instructional Material (Worksheet 2) – This worksheet supports the design and development of the instructional material. Students organize the learning experience by selecting appropriate instructional strategies, integrating relevant technological tools, and developing a prototype of the instructional material. The design emphasizes meaningful technology integration, learner engagement, and inclusive practices that respond to diverse learner needs and contexts.
  3. Implementing, Evaluating, and Improving Instructional Material (Worksheet 3) – This worksheet helps students evaluate the effectiveness of the instructional material they developed. Students simulate or plan the implementation of the material, gather feedback through evaluation tools, and analyze its strengths and areas for improvement. Based on the evaluation, students refine the instructional material and reflect on how to improve it to better support learner engagement and the achievement of learning outcomes.

The Professional Significance of the TEIM

        The development of TEIM provides pre-service teachers with opportunities to apply principles of instructional design, technology integration, and inclusive pedagogy in creating meaningful instructional material. The professional significance of the TEIM includes the following competencies aligned with the Philippine Professional Standards for Teachers[2]:

  • designing instructional material aligned with the curriculum standards and competencies (PPST 4.5);
  • using ICT positively to support teaching and learning (PPST 1.3);
  • selecting and developing appropriate instructional material for diverse learners (PPST 4.5)
  • Designing inclusive and learner-centered learning experiences (PPST 3.1)
  • Reflecting on and improving instructional practices (PPST 7.4)

Through the TEIM, pre-service teachers demonstrate their ability to design effective, inclusive technology-supported learning experiences aligned with professional teaching standards.

LEARNING OBJECTIVES

By completing the TEIM, the students should be able to:

  • analyze the learning needs and context to inform the design of the TEIM;
  • identify the intended learning outcomes and appropriate evidence of learning aligned with curriculum standards;
  • design and develop an inclusive instructional material that meaningfully integrates TPACK;
  • evaluate and improve the instructional material based on feedback and established evaluation criteria; and
  • collaborate effectively with group members to plan, develop, and present the TEIM.

EXPECTATIONS FOR GROUP WORK

The development of TEIM is a collaborative professional task. Each group is expected to work collaboratively to analyze the instructional context, design the instructional material, and refine the final output.

Each group must:

  • engage in collaborative planning and discussion;
  • reach shared decisions on key instructional elements;
  • support design choices using relevant theory, principles, or policy;
  • maintain alignment across context, outcomes, strategies, and assessment;
  • use professional and academic language; and
  • ensure meaningful contribution from all members.
