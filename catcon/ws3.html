TEIM Worksheet 3

ASSESSING, IMPROVING, AND ADVANCING INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIAL

PURPOSE: This worksheet guides your group in evaluating the TEIM's effectiveness. The focus is on assessing how well the instructional material supports the intended learning outcomes, identifying areas for improvement, and refining the design to support inclusive and meaningful learning more effectively.        

        Instructional material becomes more effective through systematic evaluation and continuous refinement. After designing the TEIM, teachers must examine how well it supports learning and identify opportunities for improvement.

        In the TUGMA instructional design framework, this stage focuses on: A - Assess, Adjust, and Advance (Ayusin, Aghamin, at Iangat ang Praktika).

        At this stage, teachers:

  • assess how well the instructional material supports learning
  • adjust the design to address gaps and weaknesses
  • advance their instructional practice through reflective improvement.

Through this process, teachers ensure that instructional materials remain aligned with learner needs, learning outcomes, and inclusive teaching practices.

This worksheet addresses the essential question: How can teachers evaluate and refine a TEIM to improve learner engagement, inclusivity, and learning outcomes?

By completing this worksheet, your group should be able to:

  1. assess the effectiveness of the instructional material using clear evaluation criteria;
  2. identify strengths and areas for improvement in the instructional material;
  3. propose revisions that enhance learning, inclusivity, and technology integration; and
  4. reflect on the instructional design process to inform future teaching practice.

This worksheet empowers you to be a reflective designer by guiding you to evaluate, refine, and improve your TEIM to support meaningful and inclusive learning better.

Processing Questions

  1. Which elements of the instructional material effectively support learner understanding?

The organization of information provides students with clear guides and instructions as they work through the content. Additionally, the use of visual elements helps make the concepts easier to understand and supports students’ comprehension while maintaining their attention. These features effectively support learners' understanding and help them acquire knowledge more comprehensively.

  1. What design features enhance learner engagement and interaction?

        The use of engaging visual elements, an organized layout, and functional instructional material makes the learning experience more engaging and interactive. Additionally, the use of an interesting theme captures learners’ attention, while the inclusion of appealing characters and features makes the material more enjoyable and insightful for students.

  1. Which aspects of the instructional material could be improved to better support diverse learners?

        Though the interactive slide instructional material is a comprehensive take in teaching Special Products and Factoring, there are still aspects that can help the instructional material improve. One of which is the implementation of an audio system, in which it can accommodate auditory learners. The use of more creative visual aids that can go beyond the limitations of the technologies used can also help learners illustrate the application of how to factor or find the special products for visual learners.

Step 2 - Adjust the Instructional Material (Ayusin ang Disenyo)

Based on the evaluation results, identify revisions that will strengthen the instructional material and improve the learning experience.

REVISION PLANNING TABLE

Identified Issue/s

Revision Strategy

Expected Impact on Learning

Learner Engagement and Contextual Relevance: The theme and concept of the TEIM may fail to capture the interest of some learners who lack prior knowledge about it.

Clear Orientation: Provide comprehensive, step-by-step instructions about the game rules, navigation, direction, and scoring mechanics at the beginning of the TEIM (Technology-Enhanced Instructional Material).

Universal Theme: Change the theme to a more universal and relatable theme that can be recognized easily by the learners.

The learners will be more motivated to participate and engage with the lessons and activities in the TEIM because the universally recognized theme makes it relatable, while the instructions, mechanics, and game rules are all clear and comprehensive.

Technology Integration: A standard Canva presentation is limiting learners to passively reading the content rather than interactive website-like features and navigation.

Creation of a Website Platform: In order to address this issue, the developers could make a website for the TEIM. It will provide more engaging website-like features and navigation, while still allowing the theme, concepts, and contents remain unique.

The learners will move from passive reading of the content to self-directed learning, enabling them to explore, master, and understand the contents deeply while enjoying learning.

Alignment with Learning Outcomes: Some sections are too wordy, making it more difficult for students to focus on mathematical concepts

Content Streamlining and Scaffolding: Trim unnecessary text and rephrase into concise and step-by-step bullet points. Designers could use visual callout boxes for key formulas and rules.

Prevents learner confusion, ensuring smooth transitions through the module so students can concentrate fully on solving problems rather than navigating the tool.

Accessibility and Inclusivity: Lengthy explanations and technical jargon could create a learning barriers to learners with different levels of comprehension

Incorporating Universal Design and Simple Language: Simplifying instructions through clear and straightforward language. Add audio narration/read-aloud options and visual representations (e.g., algebra tiles or geometric diagrams) for key concepts.

It will accommodate not just different levels of comprehension in terms of visual readings but it can also target the auditory learners that ensures equitable access and inclusivity in learning mathematical concepts

Processing Questions

  1. How will the revisions improve learner engagement?

        The revisions suggest clear, different strategies to help learners move from passively navigating to active engagement within the TEIM (Technology-Enhanced Instructional Material). These revisions also allow learners to relate themselves with the theme, explore more about the core concepts, and enjoy the activities while mastering their knowledge and skills.

  1. How will the revisions strengthen alignment with the learning outcomes?

Through removing extraneous text and cognitive barriers that distract from the core mathematical content, we can ensure that revisions directly align with learning outcomes. By rephrasing and reconstructing text into scaffolded steps and incorporating explicit visual diagrams for special products and factoring, students could focus directly on actual learning by means of observing, analyzing, and applying correct algebraic techniques. Moreover, enhancing navigation and ensuring accessibility are both necessary in such a way of accommodating students regardless of their learning styles and technology familiarity. It opens doors for students to seamlessly progress through the guided activities and complete the problem-solving assessments.

Step 3 - Advance Instructional Practice (Iangat ang Praktika)

        Reflect on the design and evaluation process. Identify insights that can inform future instructional material development and teaching practice.

INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIAL IMPROVEMENT TABLE

Reflection or Insight

Implication/s for Future Instructional Design

Accessibility

Assess what your students need in learning. In developing instructional material, it is important to know what hinders your students from learning. Keeping yourself updated to what your student is interested in can also help when thinking of ways to keep them interested in learning. In both ways, you could create a material where it is more fitting and supports their learning style. Accessibility is all about making sure that a student learns the topic.

Gender Inclusivity

In making instructional design, use words and examples that avoid stereotypes and show respect for all genders.

Processing Questions

  1. What did your group learn about designing TEIMs?
  • There are a lot of factors to consider when developing and designing TEIMs. It isn’t simply giving instructions and/or activities towards the students. The sole purpose of TEIMs is to help students actually learn the topic whether on their own or guided with a teacher using the material. Students must be able to process the information contained and the material must be able to ensure that the student gains mastery by providing activities or exercises. TEIMs are considered as a teacher’s support, and sometimes a guide, towards one goal–for students to learn.
  1. How will this experience influence your future instructional design practice?
  • This experience will influence us to be more thoughtful and careful in designing our instructional materials. Instead of focusing solely on the content of our lesson, we will also consider several factors like the abilities of our students and their different needs. We will make sure that our TEIM is engaging and is easy to understand. With that, we need to make sure that the instructions we provide are clear and are easy to follow  by  avoiding the use of jargon.  We will also use appropriate examples and activities that not only can help our students learn the lessons but also show respect and make all of the students included.

GROUP REFLECTION

  1. What was the most important insight your group gained from evaluating the developed TEIM?

The most important insight our group has made throughout the implementation of TEIM is that effective instructional design goes beyond in terms of designs, and visually appealing concepts, instead it is a balance between the usability, learning relevance, and accessibility. While incorporating engaging themes Blue Lock can capture interest, true learning occurs when the learning goal must greatly be emphasized. The successful TEIM must be flexible enough to accommodate diverse learning needs of the students without allowing the technology to become a distraction.

  1. Which improvement made the greatest difference in strengthening the instructional material?

The improvement that made the greatest difference along the way is that the group has transitioned from a presentation format toward a website featuring the learning goals, visual concepts, and the need for Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles. With the use of concise explanation, simplifying complexities such as navigation into clear, step-by-step guides, major learning hindrances are minimized. This enabled learners of all backgrounds to focus on mastering the main topic itself independently and effectively.
